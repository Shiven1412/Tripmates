Create a world-class, premium, multi-page web and mobile application called "TripMates".

Tagline:
"We don't match destinations. We match travelers."

PRODUCT VISION

TripMates is a social travel platform that helps users find compatible travel companions based on personality, lifestyle, travel preferences, budget, and interests.

The platform combines the best aspects of:
- Airbnb
- Notion
- Instagram
- Splitwise
- LinkedIn
- Tinder

This is NOT a booking website.

It is a social-first travel ecosystem centered around:
- Compatibility
- Trust
- Community
- Safety
- Collaboration
